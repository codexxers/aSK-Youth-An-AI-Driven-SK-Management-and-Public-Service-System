## 4. CHAPTER 1 — READY, SET, GO

### 4.1 Welcome

Welcome to aSK Youth. This platform is built for everyone in Barangay Concepcion Dos. Youth residents stay updated on events. SK Officers manage activities, track attendance, and draft documents. System Admins monitor performance and manage users. In this chapter, you learn how to set up your account and navigate the interface.

### 4.2 What You Need Before You Start

You need a modern web browser like Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari. You also need a stable internet connection. A speed of 10 Mbps is recommended. You need a valid email address to register. 

*Note: SK Officers must use their official SK-issued credentials. There is no software to install.*

### 4.3 Accessing the System (Youth)

There is no need to register or create an account if you are a youth resident of Barangay Concepcion Dos. A guest user account is available by default for your convenience.

**To access the system as a Youth:**

1. Open your browser and go to **[system URL]**.
2. Click the **Continue as Youth** button (A) on the login screen.
3. You will immediately access the Youth Dashboard.

*[SCREENSHOT NEEDED: Login screen with callout letter A on "Continue as Youth" button]*

### 4.4 Logging In (SK Officers & Admins)

Official accounts for SK Officers and the Chairperson are securely provisioned by the System Admin. 

**To log in to your official account:**

1. Go to the login page at **[system URL]**.
2. Enter your assigned username (A).
3. Enter your password (B).
4. Click **Sign In** (C).

*Note: Entering the wrong password 5 times locks your account for 15 minutes.*

*[SCREENSHOT NEEDED: Login screen with callout letters A on Username field, B on Password field, C on Sign In button]*

### 4.4.1 Account Management (Admins)

Account management is strictly handled by System Administrators. To ensure security, creating privileged accounts (like a new Admin or SK Officer) requires a special **Authentication Code** (admin token).

**To create a new user (Admin only):**
1. Navigate to **User Management** in the admin dashboard.
2. Click **Add New User**.
3. Fill out the user details and assign a role.
4. If creating a privileged account, enter the required **Authentication Code** (token) to authorize the creation.
5. Click **Create User**.

### 4.5 Getting Around the Interface

The interface keeps important tools one click away.

1. Use the **Sidebar Navigation** (A) to switch between modules.
2. Check the **Notification Bell** (B) for recent updates.
3. View your current access level on your **Role Badge** (C).
4. Click the **Sign Out** button (D) when you finish.

## 6. CHAPTER 3 — PROBLEM, MEET SOLUTION

### 6.1 Problems That Prevent You from Logging In

- **Forgotten password:** Please contact the System Administrator to request a password reset.
- **Account locked:** The system locks accounts for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. Please wait 15 minutes before trying again.
- **Browser cache issues:** Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then reload the page.

### 6.2 Problems with the AI Chatbot

- **Slow response time:** The AI relies on a cloud API. It requires a stable internet connection. If inactive for a while, it may take longer to wake up. Wait a few seconds for the connection to establish.
- **Language switching:** The AI speaks English and Filipino depending on your question. This is expected behavior. Ask your question in English to get an English reply.
- **Generic answers:** Provide more context. Upload relevant documents so the AI can use them.
- **Uploaded file not recognized:** Ensure your file is a standard PDF or DOCX under the size limit.

### 6.3 Problems with QR Code Scanning

- **Camera permission denied:** Check your browser settings and allow camera access for the site.
- **Invalid QR code:** Ensure you are scanning an official aSK Youth event code.
- **"Already recorded" message:** You scanned the code twice. Your attendance is already safely recorded.

### 6.4 Problems with Reports and Exports

- **Empty report:** Ensure you selected a date range that contains actual data or events.
- **Export timed out:** Large datasets take time. Refresh the page and try a narrower date range.
- **Wrong format:** Check your format selection (PDF vs. DOCX) before clicking Generate.

### 6.5 Keeping Your Account Secure

- **Use a strong password:** Combine letters, numbers, and symbols. 
- **Keep email current:** Update your email in Profile Settings if you change providers.
- **Sign out:** Always click Sign Out when using a shared or public computer.

### 6.6 Learning More, Service, and Support

For questions about programs and policies, visit the SK of Barangay Concepcion Dos at the Barangay Hall. 

For technical support regarding the system, contact the development team at their official support email.

## 7. CHAPTER 4 — LAST, BUT NOT LEAST

### 7.1 Important Safety Information — Data Privacy

Never share your account password with anyone. Use aSK Youth only on trusted devices and secure networks. Please review the privacy notice presented during registration. All data is processed in compliance with RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

### 7.2 Important Handling Information — Account Etiquette

Keep all uploaded files and messages appropriate for official SK business. Do not upload sensitive personal information to the chatbot that you would not want recorded. Always log out when you finish your session.

### 7.3 Understanding Accessibility and Ergonomics

For a comfortable experience, maintain good posture while using your computer. You can adjust your browser zoom or font size using standard browser shortcuts. The system is mobile-responsive and adapts cleanly to your phone or tablet.

### 7.4 aSK Youth and the Environment

By handling documents, reports, and attendance digitally, aSK Youth supports paperless operations. This modern approach reduces waste and aligns with the barangay's sustainability goals.

### 7.5 Regulatory and Compliance Information

This system enforces strict data protection compliance under RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012). Its budgeting and administrative features align with RA 10742 (SK Reform Act). The platform was evaluated and verified against the ISO/IEC 25010 quality framework.
